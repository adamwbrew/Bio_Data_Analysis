Do PLA size and shape help explain the difference?

Morphology means size and shape. The model groups PLA detections with similar measurements into three clusters, C1–C3. These are descriptions of measured objects, not cell types. Read each PLA arm separately.

Exploratory results · six animals, three per group. The model does not use running status, ChAT positivity or location to form these groups. Comparisons give each animal equal weight. Use the patterns to guide image review; they do not prove a causal running effect.

How to use these views, in three steps
  1. Locate a pattern. Choose a PLA arm, animal and cluster. The map shows where that cluster contributes a larger or smaller share of PLA detections; the adjacent plot shows its size and shape.
  2. Compare animals. Read the mixture and ChAT-association plots together. Then check whether matching the mixture changes the group difference, and whether omitting an animal changes the learned groups.
  3. Inspect a source object. Select an image-review target above the map. The cross marks its location; the source CSV, object ID and review window appear below. Download the target table to request the corresponding microscopy crop.

What size and shape define the clusters?

Each dot is a sampled PLA detection from this animal. Right = larger area; higher = more similar minimum and maximum diameters. Colors identify clusters; the selected cluster is emphasized.

Does the size-and-shape mix differ with running?

Cluster share = PLA in that cluster ÷ all eligible PLA in the animal. Eligible means usable size-and-shape measurements. Every dot is an animal; short lines show equal-animal group means. The three cluster shares total 100% for each animal.

Within a cluster, how often is PLA ChAT-positive?

Association = ChAT-positive PLA in that cluster ÷ all PLA in that cluster. This denominator differs from cluster share. Compare the animal dots and group means; a common cluster need not have high ChAT association.

Does a comparable morphology mix remove the gap?

The adjusted comparisons give every animal the same cluster proportions. Compare the gap before and after this adjustment, including an area-only check and 2–4 clusters. If the gap remains, matching these measured features has not removed it.

Would omitting one animal change the learned groups?

Refit using five animals, then compare cluster assignments for the omitted animal with the full-data model. Lower agreement flags a section to inspect. High agreement means stable assignments, not a validated prediction.

Definitions, normalization and technical limits

What the model learns. Each arm has its own model using log-transformed PLA area and the shape ratio, minimum diameter ÷ maximum diameter. Equal-sized animal samples are used for fitting; feature scaling is learned from those samples. C1–C3 rank the fitted cluster areas within one arm. C1 in HK1 is not established as the same biological class as C1 in PFKP.

What the percentages mean. Cluster share divides by all eligible PLA in that animal; within-cluster association divides by PLA in that cluster. Group means weight animals equally. The common-mixture check applies the same supported cluster weights to every animal; it does not correct tissue area, ChAT availability, acquisition, segmentation or section sampling. A percentage-point difference is a subtraction of percentages: 50% − 40% = 10 percentage points. Reversing the subtraction changes its sign, not its evidence.

What the maps show. Native means original export coordinates. Every square is a 500 × 500-unit bin. Cluster share needs at least 10 eligible PLA detections in the bin; the exclusion map divides excluded detections by all PLA and needs at least 10 total detections. Gray means observed PLA with insufficient support; white means no PLA detections. Neither establishes missing or absent tissue. Coordinates are uncalibrated. No smoothing, anatomical registration, cross-animal overlay or tissue-area normalization is applied here.

What to be cautious about. Unusable morphology is excluded from this model only and counted in the audit; the primary unfiltered analysis retains it. A reported zero minimum diameter can reflect export precision or segmentation, so it is not proof of biological elongation. Clusters and review targets are exploratory. Confirm source-image quality and obtain additional independent animals before predictive modeling or confirmatory testing.
